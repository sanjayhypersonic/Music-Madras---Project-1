| R-21 | **Recording-level holds (implemented 2026-10-24, committed as a local checkpoint).** A hold is four nullable columns on `recordings` (reason RIGHTS/COPYRIGHT/PRIVACY/OTHER, note, time, who; a CHECK keeps them all-or-none), imposed and lifted on Review & Publish by anyone who may publish (`RECORDING_PUBLISH`, checked again on the server, like a video hold), each with a `RECORDING_HOLD_SET` / `RECORDING_HOLD_LIFTED` audit entry in the same transaction under the per-Recording edit lock. It changes no status, pointer or revision, and lifting it publishes nothing. **Enforced in the canonical readers:** `isRecordingPubliclyVisible` (published AND not held) and its SQL twins `RECORDING_NOT_HELD_WHERE` / `PUBLIC_RECORDING_WHERE` are used by the Recording page and lookup, archive search (rows and total), the sitemap, related content on every entity page, collections, learning guides, Occasion pages and the video page's link to its Recording; `PUBLISHED_REVISION_WHERE` is hold-aware, so a held Recording also stops lifting its performance, programme, venue, participants, work and composer into discovery (they stay discoverable through any other public content), exactly as unpublishing does. A source-level test fails if a public file goes back to the publication-only predicate. **Decisions and assumptions:** (1) a hold is independent of video holds in both directions; the video's own page is unaffected, but its link to a held Recording is removed.
